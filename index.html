How movement works

  • Phrases live in one box for 2–4 bars, then slide to a neighbor (1↔2↔3↔4↔5).
  • Pentatonic notes are held; the extra 2 / 6 / ♭6 pass through.
  • The gold tab cell is the sounding note. The view scrolls to it.
